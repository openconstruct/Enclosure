Proofing sourdough: temperature and time

Most sourdough doughs proof best between 24 and 26°C. At that temperature a
bulk ferment takes about 4 to 5 hours with a 20% starter. Below 22°C it slows
noticeably; above 28°C it speeds up and turns more sour. A retard in the
fridge at 4°C overnight gives more flavour and an easier morning bake.
